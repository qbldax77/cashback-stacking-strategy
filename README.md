## **What Is Cashback Portal Stacking and How Does It Actually Work?**

Cashback portal stacking is the practice of combining multiple independent reward sources on a single purchase so the returns add together rather than cancel each other out. ## **Common Stacking Mistakes (And How to Avoid Them)**

Understanding cashback stacking mechanics is only half the battle. Execution matters equally.

**Mistake 1: Using unauthorized coupon codes.** TopCashback clearly lists which coupon codes are compatible with cashback. Applying codes outside this list can trigger terms violations and forfeit your entire cashback credit. Always use only the codes TopCashback explicitly recommends.

**Mistake 2: Allowing browser interference.** Ad blockers, privacy extensions, or browser tab-switching can interrupt tracking cookies and cause cashback to fail to register. Use a clean browser session, disable ad blockers during TopCashback purchases, and avoid jumping between tabs.

**Mistake 3: Switching between portals mid-purchase.** If you click through TopCashback, then change your mind and visit Rakuten's portal on the same purchase, the second portal overwrites the first portal's tracking cookie. Only one portal can track a single purchase. Choose based on rates and commit.

**Mistake 4: Ignoring merchant fine print.** Major retailers like Best Buy advertise flashy rates (15% cashback) that often apply only to specific categories or limited-time promotions. Read the fine print before clicking through—the advertised rate might not apply to your intended purchase.

**Mistake 5: Forgetting gift card terms.** Cashback often doesn't apply to gift card purchases or purchases made *with* gift cards at certain retailers. Always check whether TopCashback tracks through a specific retailer's gift card purchases before activating.

**Mistake 6: Failing to document high-value purchases.** For large purchases ($100+), take screenshots of the TopCashback offer, your order confirmation, and your receipt. If cashback doesn't track (rare but possible), having documentation makes filing a claim substantially easier.

**Mistake 7: Not timing purchases around promotional windows.** TopCashback runs seasonal promo weeks—Black Friday in July, Travel Week, holiday promotions—where rates spike to 10%, 15%, 20% or higher at major retailers. Timing flexible purchases around these windows amplifies returns dramatically.
